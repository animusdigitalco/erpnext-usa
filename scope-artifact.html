Scope · Pre-build · 2026-10-29

ERPNext USA

An overlay app and distribution that makes a fresh ERPNext install feel like American accounting software out of the box. Same engine, opinionated defaults, a name people can say.

Awaiting 8 decisions 33–44 build days 3 phases, each shippable GPL-3.0 overlay

Thesis

ERPNext loses nearly every US small-business evaluation in the first hour, and it loses on configuration, not capability. The product ships India-first and the US story in core is almost nothing. We package the missing hour, and every install becomes an Animus implementation lead.

Evidence

Verified against frappe/erpnext version-15 on 2026-10-29, not recalled:

  • No verified US chart of accounts in core. France, Belgium, Brazil, francophone Canada and Burkina Faso (twice) all have one. The United States has zero.
  • The entire US regional module is one 1099 supplier checkbox, two sales-tax-exempt flags, and an IRS 1099 report.
  • Zero public TaxJar, Avalara, or NACHA apps exist on GitHub. Three one-star Plaid experiments (one active: aerele, pushed 2026-06).
  • Open-source bank feeds are an unfilled gap — frappe/banking is a private paid app.
  • Core is healthy (40.7k stars, ~2.5k PRs merged in two months), so the play is a distribution that tracks upstream, never a divergent fork. The top-voted open issue has 18 reactions; real demand lives on the forum and in lost evaluations.

Architecture — three repos

  • Tracking fork of frappe/erpnext under animusdigitalco. Zero divergent commits, auto-synced. Exists for discoverability and emergency pins. Never the product.
  • erpnext_usa — the overlay app. Everything ships as fixtures, custom fields, hooks, regional overrides and a setup-wizard hook. Nightly CI installs against v15 and v16 so upstream churn surfaces within a day.
  • Distribution repo — one-command install, docker compose, seeded demo company ("Prairie Supply Co."), docs, and a bundle manifest that offers erpnext_debug, erp-portal-branding and erpnext-assistant as extras.

The phases

Phase 1 · Credibility releaseUS defaults

10–14 build days

A fresh site an American bookkeeper recognizes.

  • Verified US chart of accounts (numbered, GAAP-style, LLC/S-corp flavor) — also submitted upstream as a PR to core: contribution, credibility and attention in one move
  • Locale defaults: MM-DD-YYYY, USD formats, US timezones, Letter print formats (core defaults to A4), US terminology fixtures
  • Workspace decluttering: India/GCC artifacts hidden, opinionated SMB layout
  • Sales tax foundation: state/county/city template structure, origin-vs-destination rules per state, taxable-shipping flags (manual tables, labeled estimates)
  • Exemption certificates: workflow, attachment, expiry reminders
  • 1099 done properly: NEC vs MISC, W-9 request and storage, year-end report + CSV
  • NACHA/ACH export: Payment Order → bank-ready file (PPD/CCD)

Phase 2 · Accuracy releaseConnected services

15–20 build days
  • TaxJar connector: live rates at quote/invoice time, filing-ready liability reports (Avalara later if demand shows)
  • Plaid bank feeds: evaluate contributing to aerele/plaid_integration before building fresh
  • Economic nexus tracker: per-state revenue/transaction thresholds with approach alerts. Nobody in the ecosystem has this; it is the headline of the launch story
  • State sales tax return summaries: collected vs owed by jurisdiction and period

Phase 3 · Attention releaseThe distribution

8–10 build days
  • One-command install + docker compose, seeded US demo company
  • Hosted demo, docs site, launch video (the assistant answering questions about US data is the demo moment)
  • Frappe marketplace listings + bundle manifest for the three existing apps
  • Launch: Frappe forum localization threads, Show HN, r/selfhosted, QuickBooks-alternative SEO content

Phase 4 · Candidates, not committedIf 1–3 validate

own scope docs
  • QuickBooks escape hatch (QBO importer: COA, customers, vendors, open invoices) — likely the single biggest adoption lever, big enough to earn its own scope
  • Gusto/ADP payroll journal import · Avalara · ACH debit · lockbox import

Out of scope, permanently

  • Payroll. US payroll is a compliance product, not a feature. Journals import from Gusto/ADP; withholding is never calculated here.
  • Direct tax filing with states.
  • GAAP inventory valuation changes, multi-entity consolidation, audited-financials features.

Naming, trademark, licensing

"ERPNext" is Frappe's trademark. Convention tolerates app names like erpnext_usa, but the distribution brand must not read as official: it gets its own name with a "built on ERPNext" tagline. Candidates to react to: Mainstreet ERP · Homestead ERP · Ledger & Main. ERPNext is GPL-3.0, so the overlay app ships GPL-3.0 (unlike the three MIT apps); the distribution repo stays MIT. No effect on the services business model.

Business model & success metrics

The software is free. Revenue is Animus: implementation, hosting, support retainers, eventually a managed tier. At 90 days post-launch: 500+ stars across the repos, 100+ documented installs, the upstream COA PR merged or in serious review, 10+ community threads, and 3+ qualified implementation leads.
